## 📐 Acoustic & CAD Specifications

- **Root Note**: MIDI 69
- **Musical Scale**: `hijaz`
- **Melody Preset**: `desert_caravan`
- **Mouthpiece Profile**: `venturi`
- **Windway Texture**: `ribbed`
- **Drone Air Balancing Ratio**: `0.75`
- **Total Height**: 389.6 mm
- **Melody Tube Length**: 359.6 mm (440.0 Hz)
- **Drone 1 Tube Length**: 354.8 mm (440.0 Hz)
- **Drone 2 Tube Length**: 234.7 mm (659.3 Hz)
- **Tone Holes**: 7 holes (337.7mm, 279.1mm, 261.8mm, 229.9mm, 215.3mm, 188.5mm, 164.6mm)
