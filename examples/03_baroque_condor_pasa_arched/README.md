# 🪈 Andean Condor Pasa Triple Flute (A4) with Arched Baroque Windway

Minor pentatonic triple flute in A4 with a crowned arched windway inspired by Baroque recorders, focusing laminar airflow onto the center of the labium for singing upper harmonics.

## 📐 Acoustic & CAD Specifications

- **Root Note**: MIDI 69
- **Musical Scale**: `minor_pentatonic`
- **Melody Preset**: `condor_pasa`
- **Mouthpiece Profile**: `arched`
- **Drone Intervals**: 0 and 7 semitones from the root
- **Total Height**: 451.0 mm
- **Melody Tube Length**: 374.5 mm (440.0 Hz)
- **Drone 1 Tube Length**: 379.0 mm (440.0 Hz)
- **Drone 2 Tube Length**: 251.7 mm (659.3 Hz)
- **Tone Holes**: 5 holes of 6.5 mm, measured from the fipple (274.1mm, 247.3mm, 217.5mm, 170.1mm, 155.4mm)
- **Tuning Residual**: worst 0.0 cents across the solved lattice
- **Hole Notice**: minor_pentatonic supplies only 5 rising tone-hole degrees; 1 of the 6 requested hole(s) repeated or fell below an existing pitch and were dropped.
